# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

FastAPI, server-rendered Jinja2, HTMX, and one hand-written CSS file with no build step.

## Users

The primary user is a person in India who found a customer-care number while handling an urgent errand, often on a phone and possibly not fluent in English. Journalists, cyber-cell officers, and brand trust-and-safety analysts are secondary users of stored city/language sweeps.

## Product Purpose

Sahi Number answers whether a number surfaced by Google is also published by the relevant brand. Success means a user can see the limited verdict, the separate signals behind it, and the stored search receipt without being given a false safety promise.

## Positioning

The search results page is itself the audited object: code collects numbers across organic results, ads, Maps, and AI answers, then compares them with brand-controlled sources.

## Operating Context

Checks happen mid-errand on mobile and must be scannable in English or Hindi. Evidence is replayable offline. Search credits are scarce and visible before and after each check.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four verdicts only: official match, not corroborated, conflict, and cannot verify. No LLM, user accounts, blended trust score, or safety/fraud label. SerpApi is the sole search-data source; only configured official contact pages may be fetched directly.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Sahi Number. The voice is direct, calm, specific about evidence, and explicit about uncertainty.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository ships synthetic fictional replay evidence. No real brand number, testimonial, outcome statistic, or endorsement may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Code decides and receipts make the decision inspectable.
- Absence is not evidence of wrongdoing.
- Signals stay separate instead of collapsing into a score.
- Budget and skipped work remain visible.
- Mobile comprehension takes priority over decorative novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Verdicts use icon shape plus text, never colour alone. The interface supports English and Hindi, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, and touch-sized controls.
