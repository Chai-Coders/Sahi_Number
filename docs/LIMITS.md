# What Sahi Number cannot tell you

- A number missing from an official site is not proof of wrongdoing.
- Branch, dealer, and franchise numbers are often absent from head-office sites.
- A brand with no verifiable website cannot be verified.
- Results reflect only what SerpApi returned for one city, language, device, and moment.
- Numbers shared through WhatsApp, Facebook, SMS, or phone calls are invisible here.
- Ads rotate; an ad seen once may not appear in a later check.
- Phone extraction can miss numbers shown only inside images.
- Official-site checks depend on the configured seed domain being correct.
- Search snippets can be stale or incomplete.
